Tennessee DOK-3 Analysis:

At 1.0 atm, water remains liquid between 273 K (0°C) and 373 K (100°C). Lowering pressure below 0.006 atm triggers sublimation directly from ice to gas!

Textbook Connect (p. 128)

Why does water boil at lower temperatures at high altitudes? Atmospheric pressure is lower on mountains, so liquid molecules need less thermal kinetic energy to overcome external pressure and escape into gas.

STEMscopes 15 Lab Guide

Standard 7.PS1.3: In this laboratory module, you investigate how heating and cooling change the motion, spacing, and state of matter.

Quick Cheatsheet:

• Solid: Tightly packed, vibrate in fixed positions. Definite shape & volume.

• Liquid: Close together, slide past each other. Definite volume, no definite shape.

• Gas: Widely separated, fast random collisions. No definite shape or volume.
