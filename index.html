What are Atoms, Elements, and Compounds?

Scientists use models to understand tiny things. We are using hardware to model how matter is built!

1. Atom

The smallest building block.

A single bolt, a single nut, or a single washer represents one individual atom.

2. Element

Made of only ONE type of atom.

If you have a pile of only washers, or two bolts stuck together, it's an element because they are identical.

3. Compound

Different atoms bonded together.

When you connect a Bolt and a Nut together, you create a compound! They make a brand new substance.
