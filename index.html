Particle-Level Phenomenon

Welcome, Scientist! Examine the particle models in the 3 research chambers below. Observe patterns in how atoms are arranged, connected, or mixed to discover how matter is classified!

Particle Chamber A

Observation 1

What do you observe?

All particles in this sample are identical in color and size. None of the particles are connected to each other.

Notice: Only one type of building block is present.

Particle Chamber B

Observation 2

What do you observe?

Different types of atoms are linked (bonded) together into identical cluster units throughout the container.

Notice: The atoms are chemically connected together.

Particle Chamber C

Observation 3

What do you observe?

Multiple different individual atoms and clusters are floating together, physically blended without connections between them.

Notice: Combination of different unbonded structures.
